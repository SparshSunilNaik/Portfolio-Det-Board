# Visual direction

THE-WEB's visual presence comes from density, overlapping material cues, aggressive contrast, and the sense that investigation continues outside the primary cards. CASE 000 keeps its clearer recruiter-oriented hierarchy while borrowing those principles rather than its content or exact composition.

Phase 3 adds:

- a compact original angular spider emblem with eight deliberate legs and a crimson evidence thread;
- stronger room vignette and localized board lighting;
- a professional case-file cluster beside the central dossier;
- low-priority registration labels, ruler marks, tape, a coffee ring, annotations, and two supporting scraps;
- differentiated employment folders, clipped research reports, taped polaroids, terminal screws, blueprint overlays, and underlined clippings;
- stronger thread hierarchy and a dotted gold `ENGINEERED_AT` connection;
- a framed, more dimensional central identity dossier and an integrated classified header plate.

Phase 4 replaces the isolated phosphor-green terminal treatment with a classified-midnight system: deep navy charcoal, steel-blue casing, icy-blue technical text, warm off-white labels, and restrained crimson markers. COBOT receives its own layered system schematic based on the verified Browser → Next.js → FastAPI → SQLite/Ollama architecture; no physical robotics imagery is used.

All additions are CSS/SVG/static markup. They add no animation loop, bitmap texture, canvas renderer, or runtime dependency.
